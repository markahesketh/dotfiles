# Buyer Insights (Data-driven)

Targeting and list-building intelligence drawn from HubSpot data. Pairs with `persona/buyer-personas.md` (full messaging) and `signal/buying-signals.md` (when each buyer enters a deal).

## Quick targeting table

| Buying centre | Indicative titles | What they react to | Lead feature interest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supply Chain / Inventory | Supply Chain Director/Manager, Inventory Control, CI/Lean, VP Ops | Accuracy, audits/compliance, cost, throughput | Integrity, Client Segmentation, Pick Face, Slotting Verification, Case Count |
| Innovation / IT | Head of Innovation, IT/Systems, WMS Lead, CTO/CIO org, Solution Engineers | Integration risk, data quality, drones/alt-tech, future-proofing | Integrity/Optimise modules, APIs, digital twin, congestion heatmaps, outbound optimiser |
| Finance / Procurement | CFO, Head of Finance, Procurement Lead | Cost, ROI, payback, disruption risk | Integrity (loss/cost), Client Segmentation (billing), Optimise (utilisation) |
| Executive / Director | COO, VP Ops, GM, MD, Director Ops | SLA risk, customer trust, multi-site scale, safety | Integrity + Optimise bundle, Slotting/Weight Restrictions, network roll-out |
| Quality / Safety (smaller) | QHSE, Quality Manager, Safety Lead, CI Manager | Overweight bays, height/clearance, compliance | Slotting Verification, Weight Restrictions, exception alerts |

## How to use for outbound list-building
- Filter for **operators** of 20,000m²+ warehouse space (3PLs, retailers, manufacturers) — not vendors selling to warehouses.
- Lead the first touch with the **Supply Chain / Ops** layer: they initiate ~80% of qualified opportunities. Open on operational pain + quick wins (see `play/lifecycle-plays.md`).
- Plan the multithread early: line up IT (integration), Finance (ROI), and an Exec sponsor — won deals consistently involve all three. See `signal/buying-signals.md`.
