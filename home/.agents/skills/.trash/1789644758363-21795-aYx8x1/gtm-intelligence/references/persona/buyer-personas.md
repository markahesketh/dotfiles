# Buyer Personas (Data-driven, HubSpot)

Five buying centres that appear in Dexory deals. Use these for targeting, list-building and tailoring the *business* case. (For day-to-day product usage and pain, use the operational personas instead.) Funnel-entry timing for each is in `signal/buying-signals.md`.

---

## 1. Supply Chain / Inventory Leader
- **Titles:** Supply Chain Director/Manager, Inventory Control, CI/Lean, Ops, VP of Operations.
- **Focus:** stock integrity and reporting — accuracy KPIs, audits, stock costs, process improvement. Success = reliable data, audit-ready, lower costs.
- **Top pains:** accuracy, audits/compliance, cost, throughput.
- **Feature interest:** Integrity, Client Segmentation (per-client KPIs & billing), Pick Face, Slotting Verification, Case Count.
- **Messaging — outcome-first:** "Inventory integrity your WMS can trust."
- **Why Dexory:** exception-based cycle counting; segment KPIs per client/brand; shrink lost/unknown stock.
- **Proof:** 35%+ fewer discrepancies when teams work from real-time data; TEI ROI.
- **CTAs:** See exactly where discrepancies are hiding in your WMS · Cut your cycle-count list down by 80% · Recover wasted hours this quarter · Save on write-offs with daily integrity · Find your cost of inventory distortion and how to fix it.

## 2. Innovation / IT
- **Titles:** Head of Innovation, IT/Systems, WMS Lead, CTO/CIO org, Innovation Manager, Solution Engineers.
- **Focus:** tech enablement and integration — deploy new tools, integrate with WMS, drive digital projects. Success = seamless data flow, stable automation, ROI delivered.
- **Top pains:** integration risk, data quality, alternative tech (drones), future-proofing.
- **Feature interest:** Integrity/Optimise modules, APIs, digital twin, congestion heatmaps, outbound optimiser.
- **Messaging — outcome-first:** "A single digital twin of locations, exceptions, and actions — API-first."
- **Why Dexory:** autonomous capture + platform analytics; stable in live ops; WMS-level match and audit trail.
- **Proof:** references, uptime, deployment in 1–2 weeks.
- **CTAs:** Review the data model & API in a 30-min technical session · See how easy it is to scale site to site · Run a PoC without disrupting your floor · See where AI adds value beyond scanning.

## 3. Finance / Procurement
- **Titles:** CFO, Head of Finance, Procurement Lead.
- **Focus:** ROI and cost control — approve spend, negotiate contracts, prove savings. Success = fast payback, lower stock loss, predictable spend.
- **Top pains:** cost, ROI, payback, risk of disruption.
- **Feature interest:** Integrity (loss/cost reduction), Client Segmentation (accurate billing), Optimise (space utilisation, throughput).
- **Messaging — outcome-first:** "219% ROI, <6-month payback, reduced stock losses and wasted labour time."
- **Why Dexory:** daily counts eliminate audits and rework; exception-led fixes cut write-offs.
- **Proof:** Forrester TEI, verified customer benchmarks.
- **CTAs:** Run your ROI scenario on your numbers · See how fast you reach payback · Eliminate annual stock-audit costs · Find out your wasted-labour cost per year · How Dexory customers avoid SLA penalties.

## 4. Executive / Director
- **Titles:** COO, VP Ops, GM, MD, Director Ops.
- **Focus:** strategic resilience and growth — oversee sites, meet SLAs, scale automation. Success = consistent results, fewer escalations, smooth rollouts.
- **Top pains:** SLA risk, customer trust, scale across sites, safety.
- **Feature interest:** Integrity + Optimise bundle, Slotting/Weight Restrictions, network roll-out.
- **Messaging — outcome-first:** "Operational resilience at scale: fewer surprises, fewer escalations."
- **Why Dexory:** real-time integrity + optimisation; quick wins in 2 weeks; scalable across regions.
- **Proof:** multi-site logos, uptime, time-to-value.
- **CTAs:** Pilot in one site → roll to three · How Dexory protects revenue by preventing disruption · How automation builds resilience into your network · How leading 3PLs/retailers win contracts with Dexory.

## 5. Quality / Safety (smaller segment)
- **Titles:** QHSE, Quality Manager, Safety Lead, Continuous Improvement Manager.
- **Focus:** compliance and risk prevention — monitor safety, ensure standards, support audits.
- **Top pains:** overweight bays, height/clearance, compliance.
- **Feature interest:** Slotting Verification, Weight Restrictions, exception alerts.
- **Messaging — outcome-first:** "Prevent incidents before they happen."
- **Why Dexory:** automated checks on weight/height constraints with immediate alerts & audit trail.
- **CTAs:** See at-risk bays flagged automatically · Get an automatic safety report of your layout · Generate audit-ready dashboards for inspection · How Dexory helps you meet client safety standards · Let your team focus on prevention, not paperwork.
