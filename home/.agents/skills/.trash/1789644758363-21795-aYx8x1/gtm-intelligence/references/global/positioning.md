# Company Positioning (Synthesis)

A short top-of-message framing synthesised from the deck's messaging layers. Use for boilerplate and quick context; defer to the persona/vertical files for tailored messaging.

## What Dexory is
Autonomous mobile robots that perform daily wall-to-wall warehouse scans, plus a software platform that builds a real-time **digital twin** of the warehouse — locations, exceptions, and recommended actions. API-first.

## Core value
Move warehouses from **reactive** (manual counts, historical reports, firefighting) to **proactive** (daily ~99.9% accuracy, exception-based action, predictive alerts). Eliminate wasted work and blind spots; protect SLAs; cut cost.

## Module families
- **Integrity** — inventory accuracy: exception-based cycle counting, Pick Face, Block Stack, Double-Deep, Case Count, Client Segmentation.
- **Optimise** — space & flow: Slotting Verification, Weight Restrictions, Congestion Analysis, Outbound Optimiser, Consolidation Planner.

## Who it's for
Operators of large warehouse space (20,000m²+): 3PLs, retailers, manufacturers — across 3PL/contract logistics, retail/eFulfilment, F&B, pharma/beauty, automotive/manufacturing, and air cargo. Not vendors selling into warehouses.

## Headline credibility
Forrester TEI: 219% ROI, <6-month payback. ~99.9% accuracy. Deployment in 1–2 weeks. Gartner Cool Vendor. Trusted by Maersk, GXO, Stellantis, Nidec, NFI, GE Appliances, Iron Mountain, Yusen and others.

## Positioning line
"Robots and AI don't replace jobs — they eliminate wasted work."
