# Operational Persona — Inbound/Outbound Manager

**One-liner:** Senior operations leader responsible for the seamless flow of product from receiving through dispatch.

## Mission statement
"I exist to ensure seamless flow of product from receiving through to dispatch, so that orders ship on time and in full while optimising labour and conveyance capacity, and I measure success by dock-to-stock within 24 hours, wave closure within 3 days and % on-time loading."

## Organisational context
- 8–15 years' warehouse experience, often promoted from supervisor. Deep knowledge across receiving, putaway, wave planning, shipping.
- Owns end-to-end inbound/outbound: labour planning, staffing/targets, bottleneck resolution.
- Reports to General Manager; works closely with Inventory Control, Picking, Transportation, Customer Service. Manages shift supervisors across inbound/outbound ops, putaway & replen, dock coordination.

## Definition of success
Dock-to-stock within 24h · on-time loading 95%+ (contractual SLA) · orders ship in-full without controllable shortages · labour utilisation optimised (UPH/TPH met) · conveyance capacity maximised without bottlenecks.

## Daily activities
- **Pre-shift (6–7am):** review overnight — wave closure, inbound receipts, labour vs. plan, aging-inventory alerts, conveyance issues.
- **7:30am floor walk:** dock doors, staffing, overnight issues; verify trailers unloading on schedule; check outbound staging for congestion.
- **8am planning:** staffing allocation, wave priorities, expected volumes; adjust for call-outs/absenteeism.
- **9am cross-functional sync:** inventory control (blocked locations), wave planning (priorities), routing (shipping deadlines).
- **Midday:** UPH/TPH by department; address underperformers; set second-half priorities.
- **End-of-shift:** shipped vs. plan, wave closure, labour utilisation, handover. Cadence: informal daily + formal weekly/monthly/quarterly.

## Primary KPIs
- Dock-to-stock time (24h from trailer arrival to allocatable)
- Wave closure (3 days from release to picked/packed/ready)
- On-time loading (95%+ contractual SLA)
- In-full shipments (complete, no controllable shortages)
- UPH / TPH (labour productivity vs. standard)
- Conveyance utilisation (% theoretical capacity without bottlenecks)

## Reporting & decisions
- Power BI: wave closure, dock-to-stock, on-time loading %, labour hours & UPH/TPH, aging inventory in staging.
- WMS: inbound receipts, putaway completion, wave release/closure, outbound shipments, conveyance flow rates.
- 6+ daily email reports; typically 20+ desktop tabs of live data.
- Decisions: labour reallocation between departments, conveyance-bottleneck response, when to escalate.

## Pain points (with impact)
- **Conveyance bottlenecks:** physical constraints cap throughput (e.g. 8-to-1 merge to shipping); 2008 infrastructure can't handle 2026 volumes. → Staging congestion, delayed shipments, wasted labour.
- **Staffing unpredictability:** plan set pre-shift, then call-outs force lean running or pulling labour across departments. → Can't plan for absenteeism; constant staffing firefighting.
- **Work-mix complexity:** "happy path" orders fluid-load directly; complex orders need palletising, pack-and-hold, multiple touches. → Work mix drives throughput more than labour/systems; hard to forecast; staging congests.
- **Fragmented reporting:** 6+ email reports, 20+ tabs, cross-referencing Power BI — no single inbound/outbound view. → 30–45 min gathering data; early-warning signals missed.
- **Wave-planning coordination:** wave closure depends on picking, packing, availability, deadlines; late waves risk cut cartons. → Constant cross-dept coordination; cascading delays; incomplete shipments.
- **Utilisation & forecasting:** forecasts miss reality; excess inventory in staging; slow movers congest picks; no proactive capacity planning. → Sudden capacity crises; rushed clear/relocate decisions; lower pick efficiency.

## Overarching need
"To orchestrate seamless product flow from dock to dock with optimal labour and capacity utilisation, so every order ships on time and in full."
