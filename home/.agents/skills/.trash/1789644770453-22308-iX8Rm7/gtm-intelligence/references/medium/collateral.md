# Collateral by Funnel Stage

What to send/attach at each stage. Pair hooks from `play/lifecycle-plays.md` with the right asset.

## SQL creation (Discovery) — spark interest, establish credibility, show quick wins
- Intro/sales deck (overview of Dexory and robots)
- Short explainer video (robot scanning, 10k locations/hr)
- Vertical one-pagers (3PL, Retail, Manufacturing, Pharma, Cargo, Food & Bev)
- Blog and thought-leadership articles
- Social-proof slides (logos, early award mentions)

## Proposal — prove integration & feasibility, build trust with mid-level decision-makers
- Sales deck → technical + business slides
- Integration overview (WMS connectivity, 2-week deployment roadmap)
- Feature deep-dive sheets and one-pagers
- Video demos (scans, exception reporting)
- Technical FAQ doc
- Deployment guides / scanning-process slides

## ROI — quantify savings, reduce risk, prove ROI
- Forrester TEI study
- Interactive ROI calculator (scenario-based by warehouse size)
- ROI slides (payback, benefit categories: labour, error reduction, stockholding)
- Cost-comparison one-pager (manual vs Dexory vs drones)
- Savings benchmarks (hrs saved, misplacements avoided, SLA improvements)
- Customer testimonials with financial metrics

## Quote → Contracting — secure exec sponsorship, demonstrate resilience, mitigate risk
- Exec one-pagers by vertical
- Pilot → Scale playbook (growth across sites/regions)
- Awards & recognition; PR coverage & media mentions
- Thought-leadership papers
- Executive summary of TEI (short, board-ready PDF)
- "Trusted by industry leaders" logo slides
