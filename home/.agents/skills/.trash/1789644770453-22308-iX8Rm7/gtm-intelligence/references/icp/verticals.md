# Vertical Playbooks (ICP)

Tailored challenge → solution → features → proof per vertical. The ICP is always an **operator** of large warehouse space (3PL, retailer, manufacturer), never a vendor selling into warehouses.

---

## 3PL & Contract Logistics
- **Challenge:** multi-client warehouses struggle with accuracy, billing transparency, and meeting SLAs across different contracts; manual counts drain resources.
- **Solution:** Client Segmentation → KPIs per client, invoice fairly. Integrity + 99.9% accuracy → real-time daily accuracy across sites, fewer disputes.
- **Features to highlight:** Pick Face / Block Stack / Double-Deep (full coverage without disruption), Client Segmentation.
- **Proof:** 450% ROI at Nidec · Forrester TEI 219% ROI, <6-month payback · 47+ hrs/week saved on empty-location checks · +5pt customer satisfaction with OTIF improvement.

## Retail & eFulfilment
- **Challenge:** high SKU counts, fluctuating demand, and fast pick faces create stockouts, mispicks, and wasted cycle counts; fulfilment speed hits customer satisfaction.
- **Solution:** Pick Face → exception-based cycle counting. Integrity → 99.9% real-time accuracy for fulfilment SLAs.
- **Features to highlight:** Pick Face + full Integrity module; Congestion Analysis + full Optimise module.
- **Proof:** 25% fewer inventory errors · 45% faster issue resolution · 205+ hrs saved annually from reduced cycle counts · 70% drop in manual investigations.

## Food & Beverage (FMCG)
- **Challenge:** perishables and high-turnover goods create blind spots in block stacks and deep racks; spoilage, waste, and compliance risk drive costs.
- **Solution:** Double-Deep, Pick Face, Block-Stack → eliminate blind spots. Shelf-life monitoring (FEFO) → prevent expired stock.
- **Features to highlight:** Integrity module (Pick Face, Block Stack, Double Deep); Optimise module (Slotting Verification).
- **Proof:** accuracy 92% → 99.9% at one site · 41% fewer manual investigations · 98% putaway accuracy · significant spoilage reduction.

## Pharma & Beauty
- **Challenge:** strict compliance, traceability, and precision; errors cause costly recalls, expired products, and failed audits.
- **Solution:** continuous cycle counting + WMS reconciliation → compliance-ready accuracy. Case Count & expiry tracking → pallet-level precision and shelf-life protection.
- **Features to highlight:** Integrity module (Case Count); Optimise module (Slotting Verification).
- **Proof:** 99.9% accuracy · 32% fewer stock-transfer requests · 45% faster discrepancy resolution · 30% reduction in expired products.

## Automotive & Manufacturing
- **Challenge:** just-in-time lines can't tolerate misplaced parts, overweight pallets, or downtime; safety and compliance risks slow production.
- **Solution:** Slotting Verification & Weight Restrictions → safe, compliant storage. Real-time reconciliation → protect line feed.
- **Features to highlight:** Integrity module (Case Count, Double Deep); Optimise module (Weight Restriction, Slotting Verification, Outbound Optimiser, Consolidation Planner).
- **Proof:** zero stock outages after 3 months · 30% fewer lost-stock incidents · 99.9% compliance visibility in pilot sites · optimised layouts reduce congestion/downtime.

## Air Cargo
- **Challenge:** cargo hubs face UTLs (unable-to-locate cargo), compliance checks, and SLA pressure under tight schedules; misplacements create costly delays and disputes.
- **Solution:** automated daily scans → instant visibility of shipments and ULDs. CMS integration + AI anomaly detection → reduce disruptions, improve throughput.
- **Features to highlight:** Integrity module + Block Stack; Optimise module.
- **Proof:** 30+ hrs saved weekly · 70% fewer reporting errors · 50% fewer UTLs · stronger SLA adherence and fewer claims.
