# Operational Persona — Operations Manager

**One-liner:** Senior operations leader responsible for the end-to-end daily execution of warehouse flow.

## Mission statement
"I exist to ensure that product flows efficiently through the warehouse from receipt to dispatch, so that customer orders are fulfilled on time and in full, at the lowest possible cost, and I measure success by throughput (UPH), employee performance and meeting all SLAs."

## Organisational context
- Typically worked up from the floor → inventory management → operations management (~10–15 years' experience).
- Owns end-to-end daily execution: inbound receipt through outbound dispatch. Manages labour planning, inventory accuracy, KPI performance.
- Manages supervisors and shift leaders across the floor (inbound, replen, outbound…).

## Definition of success
SLAs met · all orders out on time and in full · zero controllable shortfall · stock loss within acceptable limits · team performance above standard · accident-free shift.

## Daily activities
- **Pre-shift:** review overnight report & KPI emails before arriving on site.
- **8am floor walk:** staffing check, surface challenges, confirm the day's targets with supervisors.
- **9am cross-functional sync:** inventory control, wave planning.
- **Continuous:** hourly target tracking, half-time meeting, end-of-shift review.

## Primary KPIs
- UPH / TPH (units / throughput per hour)
- Employee performance % vs. standard; dock-to-stock (24hr target)
- On-time loading / SLA attainment (contractual 95%+ goal)
- Block / lost inventory % (target <0.3%); order shortfall

## Reporting & decisions
- Tools: Power BI dashboards, WMS reports, Labour Management system.
- 6+ subscribed email reports daily; ~20 desktop tabs of data tracked through the day.
- Decisions: labour reallocation, wave prioritisation, problem-solving delays.
- Reports performance to site manager/director informally daily; formal weekly/monthly/quarterly.

## Pain points (with impact)
- **Data & visibility:** every shift starts with 30–45 min of manual report-checking across WMS, Power BI, email and labour reports. → Decisions delayed at shift start; KPI-drift and staffing gaps missed until they become problems.
- **Process inefficiency:** firefighting consumes most leadership time; recurring issues resurface with no time/tooling for root cause. → Performance plateaus; improvement work deprioritised; leaders permanently reactive.
- **Technology barriers:** WMS/BI show history but no predictive/AI layer — no proactive alerts, no root-cause, no auto-flagging. → Issues caught late; investigations manual and slow.
- **Communication & coordination:** cross-shift handover is verbal/manual; overnight decisions and anomalies aren't systematically surfaced. → Each shift starts from scratch.
- **Time & workload:** demand and complexity rising faster than infrastructure/tooling. → Leaders stretched thin; future-proofing deprioritised; burnout risk.
- **Inventory lifecycle is reactive:** ageing/slow stock only actioned near capacity, forcing rushed clearance. → Sudden capacity crises; congested pick areas; longer travel time.

## Overarching need
"To have the ability to make confident, proactive decisions that allow me to efficiently run my warehouse not only today, but for the future."
