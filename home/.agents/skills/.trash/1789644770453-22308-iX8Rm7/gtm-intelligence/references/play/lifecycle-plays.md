# Lifecycle Plays & Hooks

Funnel-stage goals, hooks, and content. Match every piece of derivative content to the stage it serves. Stage-entry signals and buyer timing are in `signal/buying-signals.md`; assets are in `medium/collateral.md`; proof is in `proof/proof-points.md`.

---

## Stage 1 — Lead / MQL (Awareness)
- **Goal:** spark interest, educate, position Dexory as a credible solution.
- **Hooks:**
  - "Inventory errors cost global retail $1.77T annually — how much are they costing you?"
  - "What's your true inventory accuracy? Most warehouses don't know."
  - "Robots / AI don't replace jobs — they eliminate wasted work."
- **Content:** thought-leadership blogs (e.g. *Need for Speed* paper, Gartner Cool Vendor mention); Forrester TEI stats (219% ROI, <6-mo payback); social snippets & infographics (blind spots in deep racking, wasted labour).

## Stage 2 — SQL / Opportunity (Consideration)
- **Goal:** show Dexory as best-fit; address pains and objections.
- **Hooks:**
  - "We'll show you your exceptions tomorrow — not after an audit."
  - "See how your WMS compares to daily autonomous accuracy."
  - "Dexory covers all layouts: Double Deep, Block Stack, Pick Face, racking…"
- **Content:** persona battlecards (Ops → labour savings; Supply Chain → accuracy KPIs; IT → API-first integration); customer case studies (Yusen, Nippon Express, Stellantis); customised ROI calculator.

## Stage 3 — Closed Won/Lost (Decision)
- **Goal:** de-risk the investment and prove ROI/payback.
- **Hooks:**
  - "Pilot in one site, baseline results in weeks."
  - "Your peers report +5pt OTIF improvements and 45% faster resolution times."
  - "Daily integrity means no more disruptive annual audits (daily wall-to-wall scans)."
- **Content:** Forrester TEI study (hard ROI proof); executive one-pagers by vertical; proof-of-concept offers.

## Stage 4 — Customer (Expansion & Upsell)
- **Goal:** drive module adoption and multi-site rollouts.
- **Hooks:**
  - "You've nailed integrity — now let's optimise flow and space."
  - "How much more value could you unlock across your network?"
  - "From one DC to global roll-out: here's the playbook."
- **Content:** feature launch packs; customer success ROI stories (e.g. Stellantis pilot → compliance wins); multi-site expansion playbooks (NFI, Yusen, GXO references).
