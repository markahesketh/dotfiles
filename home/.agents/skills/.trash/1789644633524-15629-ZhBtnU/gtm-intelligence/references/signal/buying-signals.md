# Buying Signals & Journey

Who initiates, when each buying centre joins, and what correlates with won vs lost — from HubSpot and lost-deal analysis. Use for multithreading guidance and deal-stage diagnostics.

## Who enters when (SQL → Contracting)
- **SQL creation (Discovery):** Ops & Supply Chain Managers are almost always first contact. ~80% of opportunities are Manager-initiated → open with operational pain and quick wins.
- **Proposal:** IT / Innovation is critical here. ~65% of won deals bring in Directors early → message must evolve to integration and strategic coverage. Most lost deals flagged integration doubts at this point.
- **ROI stage:** Finance / Procurement appears. They join in 50–60% of successful deals → without strong ROI proof, deals stall.
- **Quote → Contracting:** Execs must be pulled in before contracting. ~65% of won deals close with a Director-level sponsor; ~70% involve C-suite. Deals without exec sponsorship often stalled.

## Won vs lost correlates (diagnostic checklist)
| Signal | Healthy (won-like) | At-risk (lost-like) |
|---|---|---|
| Initiator | Ops/Supply Chain Manager engaged on operational pain | Stuck single-threaded with initiator only |
| IT involvement | Integration addressed early, Director engaged | Integration doubts unresolved at proposal |
| Finance involvement | ROI proven on their numbers, 50–60% engagement | ROI not proven strongly |
| Exec sponsorship | Director sponsor (~65%) + C-suite (~70%) | No exec buy-in before contracting |

## Action rules
- If only the initiator is engaged past discovery → push to multithread into IT and an exec.
- If at proposal and IT hasn't validated integration → book the 30-min technical/API session.
- If approaching quote without an exec sponsor → flag the deal as at-risk and engage one before contracting.
