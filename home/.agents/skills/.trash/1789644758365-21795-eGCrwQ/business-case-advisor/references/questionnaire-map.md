# Site Questionnaire: Question Map & Coverage Analysis

Source: Dexory Business Value Site Questionnaire, April 2026

---

## Full Question List

**Section 1 — Warehouse Infrastructure**
- 1.1 Warehouse size (sq ft or sq m)
- 1.2 Number of pallet locations
- 1.3 % of locations that are fully palletised
- 1.4 % of pick face locations
- 1.5 % of block or floor stack locations
- 1.6 Number of inbound pallets per day
- 1.7 Current inventory accuracy level

**Section 2 — Cycle Counting**
- 2.1 FTEs dedicated to cycle counting (day & night)
- 2.2 Fully burdened cost per cycle counter per year (pay, equipment, overheads)
- 2.3 Time to complete a full cycle count of the warehouse

**Section 3 — Audits**
- 3.1 Number of full wall-to-wall inventory counts per year
- 3.2 Days of reduced or halted operations needed to prepare
- 3.3 Total cost of audit process (internal labour + external fees)

**Section 4 — Lost / Misplaced Inventory**
- 4.1 Pallets or cartons lost or misplaced per year (or YTD)
- 4.2 Dollar/pound value of inventory written off due to loss per year
- 4.3 Time for a typical lost pallet/carton investigation
- 4.4/4.5 Deadline for locating missing inventory before write-off [duplicate question in questionnaire]

**Section 5 — Stockholding**
- 5.1 Average cost of holding inventory at site per year (or per week/month)
- 5.2 Average stockholding period per pallet (weeks)
- 5.3 Frequency of stock replenishment review

**Section 6 — Order Fulfilment**
- 6.1 Outbound orders per year
- 6.2 Current OTIF fulfilment rate
- 6.3 Estimated cost of a fulfilment error (chargebacks, re-ships, credits)
- 6.4 Hidden costs to maintaining fulfilment rate (overtime, workarounds)

**Section 7 — Storage Health**
- 7.1 Racking inspection frequency and by whom
- 7.2 Hours per week inspecting racking, pallets, packaging for damage
- 7.3 Annual spend on racking repairs or replacements
- 7.4 Average cost to repair or replace a single damaged rack bay
- 7.5 Time to identify root cause of racking damage; % of cases resolved
- 7.6 Compliance / safety penalties (HSE fines, insurance)

**Section 8 — Empty Location Management**
- 8.1 Hours per week checking for empty or misallocated locations

**Section 9 — Escalations & Errors**
- 9.1 Inventory-related escalations per week
- 9.2 Average cost per inventory error or escalation

## Known Questionnaire Gaps (benefits not covered)

The following are NOT covered by any current questionnaire question:

1. **Peak labour / overtime costs** — Relevant to the unquantified "no overtime/temp labour" benefit. Could be highly material for seasonal operations (e.g. retail 3PLs). Consider adding: *"What is your average spend on overtime or temporary labour during peak periods per year?"*

2. **Number of customers per warehouse** — Critical for the Reduced Stockholding benefit (which is really a revenue/capacity uplift for 3PLs). The questionnaire doesn't ask this, but the TEI composite assumes 3 customers/warehouse. Consider adding: *"How many customer accounts does this warehouse serve?"*

3. **Revenue per warehouse / per site** — Needed to size the stockholding benefit properly (TEI uses $10M revenue/warehouse). Not in questionnaire.

4. **Current use of forklifts for cycle counting** — The TEI highlights forklift elimination as both a cost saving and H&S benefit. Consider adding: *"Do cycle counters currently use forklifts or elevated access equipment?"*

5. **Employee turnover rate in warehouse roles** — The TEI claims 99.4% retention but doesn't quantify the financial benefit. If a prospect has high churn, this could be material. Consider adding: *"What is your approximate annual staff turnover rate in warehouse operative roles?"*

6. **WMS type and integration readiness** — Affects implementation cost and timeline. No questions on this. Consider adding: *"Which WMS do you use, and how mature is your current barcode/label quality?"*

7. **Block stack proportion** — Q1.5 asks %, which is good. But there's no follow-up on whether block stacks are currently scanned at all. This affects the size of the addressable benefit (Dexory has less visibility into block stacks).

8. **Insurance costs tied to racking/inventory** — Q7.6 asks about compliance penalties but doesn't ask about insurance premiums directly. This can be significant.
