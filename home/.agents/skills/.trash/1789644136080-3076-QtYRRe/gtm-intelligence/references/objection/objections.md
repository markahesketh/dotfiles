# Objections & Responses

Derived from the deck's won/lost journey analysis and the "Why Dexory" messaging — not a verbatim slide. Treat the recurring lost-deal themes as validated; the suggested responses are assembled from the deck's own proof and messaging.

## The three recurring lost-deal themes
1. **Integration doubt** — most lost deals flagged integration concerns (raised by IT/Innovation at proposal).
2. **Unproven ROI** — many losses happened when ROI wasn't proven strongly (Finance/Procurement stage).
3. **No executive sponsorship** — deals without an exec sponsor often stalled before contracting.

## Objection → response

| Objection | Who raises it | Response (grounded in deck) |
|---|---|---|
| "Will this actually integrate with our WMS?" | IT / Innovation | API-first digital twin; WMS-level match and audit trail; stable in live ops; deployment in 1–2 weeks. Offer a 30-min technical session on the data model & API, and a PoC that doesn't disrupt the floor. |
| "How do we know the ROI is real?" | Finance / Procurement | Forrester TEI: 219% ROI, <6-mo payback. Run the ROI calculator on their numbers. Named proof: Nidec 450% ROI / 2-mo payback; GXO 47 hrs/week + 2 FTEs reallocated. Daily counts eliminate annual audits and rework. |
| "Why not just use drones?" | IT / Ops | DCL: 10× faster than drones, 16 hrs/day saved. JAMCO ran the comparison and chose Dexory on safety + accuracy. See `alternative/alternatives.md`. |
| "Can't we keep doing manual counts?" | Ops / Finance | Manual counts drain resources and only sample; Dexory does daily wall-to-wall at ~99.9%. Yusen 205+ hrs saved; Ziegler 92% less stocktake effort. |
| "Will it disrupt live operations?" | Ops / Exec | Autonomous capture stable in live ops; pilot one site → roll to three; baseline results in weeks. |
| "Is it proven at our scale / across sites?" | Exec / Director | Maersk 100k+ pallets/day; multi-site rollouts at NFI, Yusen, GXO; uptime and time-to-value references. |

## Pre-empt, don't react
- Surface integration detail to IT **before** proposal.
- Bring ROI proof to Finance **early**, on their numbers.
- Multithread to an exec sponsor **before** contracting (won deals: ~65% Director sponsor, ~70% C-suite involved). See `signal/buying-signals.md`.
