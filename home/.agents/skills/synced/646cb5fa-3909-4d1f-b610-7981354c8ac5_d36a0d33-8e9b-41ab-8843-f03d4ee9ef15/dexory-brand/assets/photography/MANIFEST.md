# Approved photography — manifest

Real, brand-approved Dexory photography (bundled in `assets/photography/`). Use these instead of
stock or generated imagery. Per brand rules, photography should be sophisticated and focused; the
robot and DexoryView shown together where possible, with authentic human interaction.

**Placement:** always crop-fill, never stretch. In pptxgenjs use `sizing:{type:"cover",w,h}`; in
docx size to the frame and crop; never set a mismatched `w`/`h`. Put white text only over a dark
area of a photo (add a navy scrim at ~30–55% for legibility) — never over a busy/bright region.

## Resolution note

Photos are bundled at **web resolution (≤1800px long edge, JPEG q82)** to keep the skill
installable — sharp for on-screen decks and for document images up to ~half-page. The pixel
dimensions listed above are the *original* aspect-ratio references; the bundled files are smaller.
For a crisp full-bleed hero at large print size, request the original high-res files from
marketing@dexory.com.
