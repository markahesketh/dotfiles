# Alternatives & Competitive Positioning

Positioning vs the status quo and substitute technologies. Assembled from the deck's comparison points (drone comparisons, "manual vs Dexory vs drones" one-pager, manual-count pains) — not a standalone slide.

## vs Manual counting (the status quo)
- **Their reality:** manual counts drain resources, sample only a fraction, surface issues late, and force disruptive annual audits.
- **Dexory line:** daily wall-to-wall autonomous scans at ~99.9% accuracy; exception-based cycle counting cuts the count list by up to 80%; no more disruptive annual audits.
- **Proof:** Yusen 205+ hrs saved; Ziegler 92% reduction in stocktake effort, 98.5% accuracy; 47+ hrs/week saved on empty-location checks.

## vs Drones
- **Their reality:** drones are a common "alt-tech" consideration for IT/Innovation buyers.
- **Dexory line:** faster and steadier in live ops, with platform analytics and a WMS-level audit trail — not just scanning.
- **Proof:** DCL 10× faster than drones, 16 hrs/day saved; JAMCO ran the comparison and chose Dexory on safety + accuracy.

## vs WMS / BI tools alone (Power BI, Tableau, LMS)
- **Their reality:** WMS/BI show historical data but no predictive/AI layer; LMS rarely real-time integrated with WMS; anomaly tools don't contextualise for warehouse ops.
- **Dexory line:** real-time digital twin of locations, exceptions and actions, with proactive alerts and root-cause — the layer these tools lack.

## Framing rule
Position against *wasted work and blind spots*, not against people. The deck's stance: "robots/AI don't replace jobs — they eliminate wasted work."
